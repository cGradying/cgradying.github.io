Features You

Made to fit you

Your text size, Reduce Motion and haptics, respected.

You since 0.1.0

Share

IntPortal follows your phone's text size, with headings capped so they still fit. Reduce Motion skips animations to their resting state.

Haptics can be turned off, and Done effects can be calmed in You.

How to use it

  1. 1Set text size and Reduce Motion in your phone's settings.
  2. 2Open You for haptics and Done effects.
