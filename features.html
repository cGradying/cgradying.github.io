Everything in IntPortal

49 features in 9 groups. Each has its own page with what it does and how to use it.

Sign-in

Meet LAR, pick your school and sign in, or look around first.

  • Meet LAR Before any form, a short talk with LAR about you and what you need. since 0.3.0
  • SIS and eMabini Sign in to the PUP SIS, eMabini or both. since 0.1.4
  • Choose your school The portal wall: SIS and eMabini first, then every PUP school A to Z. since 0.2.0
  • Is the SIS up? A chip under the portal says when the SIS isn't answering. since 0.2.0
  • Peek inside Tap the portal to see Today, Schedule, Grades and Study with sample data. since 0.1.9
  • Look around first Open the whole app with sample classes and grades, no account needed. since 0.2.0
  • Sign-in that waits Three tries in 15 minutes, then it waits. It never retries a wrong password on its own. since 0.1.3
  • The dive Signing in dives you through the portal into your day. since 0.1.9

Today

What's next, what's due and one nudge, at a glance.

  • Up next Your next class with a countdown, or "in session" while it runs. since 0.1.0
  • Your campus, in pixels A pixel scene of your own campus while a class is in session. since 0.2.1
  • Due One list for deadlines from your syllabus, eMabini and your own. since 0.1.9
  • Add a deadline Type it, paste a list or scan it from a photo. since 0.1.9
  • LAR's nudge One factual tip at a time: a deadline, cards due or a free gap you can use. since 0.1.0
  • Works offline After one sign-in, your schedule and grades open without a signal. since 0.1.0

Schedule

Your week, with your own marks on it.

  • Week and Day Your classes on a pixel week, or one day in detail. since 0.1.0
  • Mark a class In person, online or vacant, for this week or every week. since 0.1.0
  • Move a class Change a class's time for one week or every week. since 0.1.0
  • Class colours Pick each subject's colour. since 0.1.0
  • Class links Save a class's meeting link; Join shows 15 minutes before. since 0.1.9

Grades

Every term, your real GPA and what you need next.

  • Every term, real GPA Every term with a units-weighted GPA. since 0.1.0
  • Trend and progress Your GPA across terms, and units done out of your program's total. since 0.2.0
  • Target GPA Set a goal and see what you need on the rest of the term. since 0.1.0
  • eMabini courses Courses, deadlines and course grades from eMabini. since 0.1.4

Study

Flashcards, notes and your syllabus, in one place.

  • Flashcard decks A deck per subject; add cards by hand or paste a list. since 0.1.0
  • Spaced repetition Cards come back right before you'd forget them. since 0.1.0
  • Three ways to study Flip the card, type the answer or pick from choices. since 0.1.0
  • The study game Streaks multiply points, quick answers earn a bonus. since 0.4.0
  • Streak and Done Cards due now, your daily streak and your last seven days. since 0.1.0
  • Notes Formatted notes per class, per day or by name, with photos. since 0.1.0
  • Syllabus and grading Paste an outline; IntPortal finds the lectures, quizzes and exams. since 0.1.0
  • Cards from a note LAR makes flashcards from a note, a PDF or a topic; you check them first. since 0.1.4

LAR

Ask LAR, on your phone. Nothing leaves it.

  • Ask LAR Ask about your classes, deadlines, grades and notes. since 0.1.3
  • Answers from what LAR knows School and everyday questions, with a tutor for homework. since 0.5.0
  • Suggestions you confirm LAR offers to add a note, a reminder or cards; nothing changes until you tap. since 0.1.3
  • Ask AI in a note Summarize, tidy or explain a note, then insert or replace. since 0.1.4
  • Why? After a wrong answer, LAR explains it from your class notes. since 0.1.4
  • Add IntAssis A one-time 1.48 GB download, made to keep going when you leave the app. since 0.1.3

You

Your look, your lock, your data.

  • Rooms and themes Pick a room, or make your own theme and share it by code or QR. since 0.1.0
  • Backup and restore Save your own work to a file and bring it back on any phone. since 0.1.9
  • App lock Face ID or your screen lock before the app opens. since 0.1.0
  • Stays on your phone No IntPortal server. Your data and logins stay on the phone. since 0.1.0
  • Made to fit you Your text size, Reduce Motion and haptics, respected. since 0.1.0

Outside the app

Reminders, your next class on the Lock Screen and your calendar.

  • Reminders Before each class, the evening before a deadline, and when cards are due. since 0.1.0
  • Next class outside the app Your next class on the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island or Android's status bar. since 0.1.0
  • Your phone's calendar Your classes and deadlines in an IntPortal calendar that stays in sync. since 0.1.0

Everywhere

Android, iPhone, your computer and the browser.

  • Android Install the APK from the download page. since 0.3.0
  • iPhone An unsigned build to sideload with AltStore or SideStore. since 0.4.0
  • Mac, Windows and Linux The same app on your computer, in a phone-sized window. since 0.4.0
  • Try it in your browser The real app on sample data, in any browser. Nothing is sent or kept. since 0.4.0
