What it's for

Students check their schedule and grades many times a day, often on weak data, while the portal itself is slow or down. IntPortal signs in once, keeps everything on the phone, and adds what the portal never had: a week you can mark up, one list of what's due, notes, decks that remember for you, and LAR, who gives one nudge at a time.

How it's built

Rust
The core: signing in, reading the portal, the encrypted store, the study scheduler, and the portal and dive art, compiled to WebAssembly.
Svelte 5
Every screen, the same on every platform.
Tauri 2
One app for iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux.
SQLCipher
Your data, encrypted on the device.
llama.cpp
LAR's assistant, running on the phone itself.
WebAssembly
The Rust core in your browser, for Try it.

Every change starts as a written spec and lands as a reviewed pull request. All of it is public on GitHub, and what's next is on the roadmap.
