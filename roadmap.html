Roadmap

What's done, what's being built, and what comes after. No dates until they're real.

Building1

Approved specs, being built now.

  • Own schedule Classes without a portal: own schedule mode, adding classes by hand, and reading a photo of a schedule on the phone Spec 061

Next4

Open work at the top of the list.

  • Deadlines in Android's calendar All-day deadlines show on the right day in Android's calendar. Issue #205
  • Quiz me from my notes Pick a note and LAR quizzes you on it. Issue #142
  • eMabini fixes Fix a rejected eMabini login without starting over, and clearer eMabini wording. Issue #130
  • Accessibility pass Every screen checked for screen readers, large text and Reduce Motion. Issue #66

Later5

Drafts and ideas for after that.

  • IntPortal in any browser, signed in The same app in any browser, signed in, with your data kept in the browser. Spec 056
  • Quiz me on a syllabus row Tap a lecture in your syllabus and study it right there. Issue #269
  • A syllabus from a course title Type a course name or paste its text and LAR drafts the outline for you to check. Issue #82
  • App Store and Play Store Install IntPortal from the stores instead of a file. Needs store accounts first. Issue #26
  • Home-screen widget Your next class on the home screen. Waits on a paid Apple developer account. Issue #24
