Campus

Stays on your phone

Your login only goes to your school.
It's kept in the phone's keychain and used only to sign in to your school's own portal.
Your data is encrypted on the phone.
Schedule, grades, notes and decks sit in an encrypted database. Each student who signs in gets their own.
There is no IntPortal server.
No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Nothing to sell, because nothing is collected.
It only talks to your school.
It signs in to your school's portal with your own login, the way your browser would, then works offline. If you add LAR's AI, it also downloads the model files from Hugging Face, sending nothing about you beyond the download itself.
Anyone can read the code.
It's published on GitHub, so anyone can check what the app does. The code on GitHub

The whole of it is in the privacy policy.
