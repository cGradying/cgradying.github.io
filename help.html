Installing it

Android

Android 7 or newer, 64-bit

  1. Tap Download for Android. Your browser saves IntPortal.apk.
  2. Open it from the notification or your Downloads.
  3. Android asks to allow installs from your browser the first time. Allow it, then tap Install.
  4. Play Protect may warn that it's an unknown app. Tap More details, then Install anyway. It says this for every app from outside the Play Store.
  5. Updates install the same way, over the old version, and keep your data.

iPhone and iPad

iOS 16 or newer, sideloaded. Needs a computer once.

  1. There's no App Store build yet: that needs a paid Apple developer account. So you sideload it.
  2. You need a Mac or Windows computer once, to set up AltStore or SideStore on your iPhone with your own Apple ID.
  3. Download IntPortal-unsigned.ipa on the iPhone and open it with AltStore or SideStore. It signs the app with your Apple ID.
  4. A free Apple ID runs at most 3 sideloaded apps at a time, and AltStore or SideStore counts as one.
  5. Refresh it every 7 days or it stops opening. AltStore refreshes when your computer is on the same Wi-Fi; SideStore refreshes on the phone itself.

Set up with AltStore or SideStore.

Mac

macOS 12 or newer

  1. Open IntPortal-mac.dmg and drag IntPortal to Applications.
  2. The first time, Control-click IntPortal in Applications and choose Open. macOS asks once because the app isn't notarised.

Windows

Windows 10 or newer, 64-bit

  1. Open IntPortal-windows.msi.
  2. If SmartScreen stops it, choose More info, then Run anyway. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate.

Linux

64-bit, with GNOME Keyring or KWallet

  1. On Debian or Ubuntu, install IntPortal-linux.deb. Anywhere else, make IntPortal-linux.AppImage executable and run it.
  2. Your login goes to the system keychain, so GNOME Keyring or KWallet must be running.

Every file, with its size and version, is on Download.

Questions

Is this official?

No. IntPortal is an independent student project. It isn't made by or affiliated with any school, and only works with your own account.

Share
Is my password safe?

It never leaves your phone except to sign in to your school's own portal. It's kept in the phone's keychain, and the app makes at most three sign-in tries every fifteen minutes so it can never lock your SIS account.

Share
Does it work offline?

Yes. After one sign-in, your schedule, grades, deadlines, notes and decks are on the phone. It syncs again when you have signal.

Share
Is it free?

Yes: free to download and use, with no ads. The code is published on GitHub so anyone can read it; it isn't open for reuse, so copying or changing it needs permission.

Share
Why isn't it on the App Store?

Publishing there needs a paid Apple developer account. Until then, you can sideload it with AltStore or SideStore: you need a Mac or Windows computer once to set it up, a free Apple ID runs at most 3 sideloaded apps, and you refresh it every 7 days. Or try it in the browser, no install.

Share
What about other schools?

More are coming. The portal wall already lists other schools' portals, and today PUP's SIS and eMabini sign in.

Share
Does the AI send my notes anywhere?

No. LAR's assistant runs on the phone itself, and you turn it on yourself. Your questions and notes stay on the phone.

Share

Something's wrong

It says to wait before signing in again

After three tries in fifteen minutes the app stops and tells you when you can try again. It never retries a rejected password on its own, so it can't get your school account locked. If you forgot the password, Forgot password opens your portal's own reset page.

Share
The SIS isn't answering

A chip under the portal says so; it checks without sending your login. Everything you already have still opens offline. Pull down on Today to sync again once the SIS is back.

Share
Reminders don't come

Turn them on in You, then check that your phone allows notifications for IntPortal in its Settings.

Share
The iPhone app stopped opening

Apps signed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Open AltStore or SideStore and refresh IntPortal; your data stays.

Share
The Mac says it can't open IntPortal

The Mac build isn't signed yet. The first time, open it from the right-click menu and choose Open.

Share
Windows says it protected your PC

That's SmartScreen, because the build isn't signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Share
Moving to a new phone

Make a backup in You and open it on the new phone, or just sign in again: your schedule and grades come back from the SIS. Your own notes and decks are only in the backup.
